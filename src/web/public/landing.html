How it works

01

Text what you need

“We're out of dish soap.” Restock picks the best match and adds it to the cart.

02

Order when you're ready

Nothing is bought until someone texts order now.

03

Everyone pays their share

Shared items split evenly. Personal items go to whoever added them.

Who pays

One main card

The group owner's card pays for each order, so nothing waits on a missing card.

Card on file: automatic

Add a card and your share is charged to it every order.

No card: pay back later

Restock texts you what you owe and a link to pay it back.

Groups and Personal

Groups

Roommates, family, a team. Add Restock to the group chat and say hi. It sets up the group by text.

Personal

Text Restock one on one for things that are just yours.

Built to be trusted

No card numbers in chat

Cards go on Stripe's secure page. Restock only keeps the last 4 digits.

You say when to buy

The AI never orders on its own. Big items need a yes first.
